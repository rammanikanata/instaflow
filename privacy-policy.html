InstaFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

InstaFlow helps creators manage Instagram accounts and configure message automations.

Information handled

When you create an InstaFlow account, we store your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. If you connect an Instagram account, we store its username, account identifier, and access token. We also store the automation keywords and reply text you configure. Instagram may send message event content and sender/account identifiers to our webhook so matching automations can run. The current application processes these events to send an automated reply; it does not save incoming message text as a message history.

How information is used

We use this information to provide account login, connect the Instagram account, manage automations, and deliver replies requested by the account owner.

Sharing and security

InstaFlow does not sell personal information. Information is sent to Meta/Instagram as needed to connect accounts and deliver replies. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Access tokens are stored by the application and must be treated as sensitive credentials.

Retention and deletion

Account, connected-account, and automation records are retained while the account is in use. To request deletion of your InstaFlow account and associated records, or to disconnect Instagram, email ramamanikantaguna@gmail.com from the account email and include “InstaFlow data deletion” in the subject. We will respond to verify the request and explain its status. Deleting InstaFlow data does not delete information held by Meta; manage that separately through Meta/Instagram.

Contact
